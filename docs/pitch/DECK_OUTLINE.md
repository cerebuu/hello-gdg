# Bisaya Crop Doctor deck outline

## Slide 1 — A photo can be the first question

- A farmer sees a change on a banana or cacao plant.
- A phone photo can start a conversation.
- Bisaya Crop Doctor gives possible guidance, never confirmation.

**Speaker note:** Open with the farmer’s moment of uncertainty. State the
disclaimer early: this is a decision aid, not a lab diagnosis.

## Slide 2 — The problem

- Crop problems are difficult to assess from a photo.
- Advice may be hard to find or hard to understand.
- A confident-sounding answer can be unsafe.

**Speaker note:** Keep the problem human and concrete. Do not claim user
numbers or measured losses.

## Slide 3 — The product

- Mobile-first flow for banana and cacao.
- Bisaya or Filipino guidance.
- Actions, escalation, copy, and printable farmer card.

**Speaker note:** Show the home screen and the language choice. Emphasize the
small number of steps.

## Slide 4 — See it work

- Upload or take a leaf or fruit photo.
- Receive a possible problem with uncertainty.
- Use live mode or a clearly labeled sample mode.

**Speaker note:** Run the prepared demo. Explain that a sample is not a
diagnosis of the uploaded image.

## Slide 5 — How it works

- The server sends a live submission to Google Gemini.
- The response is returned in a structured format.
- The API key stays on the server; sample mode avoids the API call.

**Speaker note:** Give enough technical detail for technical judges without
turning the slide into an architecture review.

## Slide 6 — Safety and honesty

- “Possible,” never “confirmed.”
- No branded chemical names or dosages.
- Low-confidence cases point to an agriculture professional.

**Speaker note:** Say what the product refuses to do. Mention the always-visible
disclaimer and the need for expert and native-speaker review.

## Slide 7 — Limits and next steps

- Live mode needs internet; language and crop guidance need review.
- Testing so far is on limited photos.
- Roadmap: source-backed knowledge, trained detector second checks, more crops
  and dialects, extension-officer review.

**Speaker note:** Confidence comes from naming the limits. Do not present an
accuracy percentage. Detector checks and the knowledge base are next
integrations for the server, not measured claims about the current deployment.

## Slide 8 — The ask

- Help review the agriculture guidance.
- Help review Bisaya and Filipino wording.
- Help us test responsibly with consented field photos.

**Speaker note:** Close with the collaboration needed to move from a careful
prototype to a useful tool for Davao farmers.
