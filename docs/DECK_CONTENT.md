# Bisaya Crop Doctor — six-slide pitch copy

The slide copy below describes the intended prototype and demo flow.
Gemini diagnosis and offline sample mode are planned; confirm they work before
presenting them as live features. The coping behavior on slide 2 is a hypothesis
to validate with farmers. No measured impact is claimed.

## Slide 1 — Bisaya Crop Doctor

- Photo-based crop disease guidance for Davao farmers in Bisaya or Filipino.
- Team: Jefferson F. Laspiñas, Mark Daniel Cona, Bruce John B. Arevalo, Szianzey Asesor, Caleb Adriel Tingson.
- SDGs: 2 Zero Hunger · 1 No Poverty · 8 Decent Work and Economic Growth.

Visual suggestion: A banana leaf and cacao fruit beside the three SDG icons.

## Slide 2 — Disease spreads while advice is out of reach

- Smallholder banana and cacao farmers in Davao struggle to identify diseases early; agronomists are scarce and expensive to reach.
- Today, farmers may rely on neighbors, guess the cause, or wait until they can reach an expert.
- Delayed or mistaken action can cost harvests and income. Local loss estimates: [NEEDS SOURCE].

Visual suggestion: A sick leaf beside a simple sequence: uncertainty → delay → lost harvest.

## Slide 3 — Clear advice in the farmer’s own language

- Photograph a sick leaf or fruit.
- Get a likely diagnosis and three action steps in Bisaya or Filipino.
- Our key advantage: advice in the farmer’s own language, with steps they can understand.

Visual suggestion: A phone mockup showing a leaf photo and three numbered action steps.

Disclaimer: This is a decision aid, not a lab diagnosis.

## Slide 4 — From photo to next steps

- 1. Select Bisaya or Filipino and choose a sick leaf or fruit photo.
- 2. Submit the photo and show the likely diagnosis, confidence, and severity.
- 3. Read the three action steps and when to contact an expert; show the labeled sample backup if needed.

Visual suggestion: Three screenshots in order: photo → result → action steps.

Disclaimer: This is a decision aid, not a lab diagnosis.

## Slide 5 — A photo in, clear guidance out

- The photo goes through our server to a Gemini vision model, which examines what it can see.
- A structured result comes back: likely problem, confidence, severity, three actions, and when to call an expert.
- If the live service is unavailable, offline sample mode shows a clearly labeled saved example, not a new diagnosis.

Visual suggestion: A simple flow: farmer’s photo → server → Gemini → result, with a separate sample-backup path.

Disclaimer: This is a decision aid, not a lab diagnosis.
