list categories, not concrete visual symptom descriptions. Do not derive lesion
shape, color, treatment, or geographic prevalence from a label alone. The Kilimo
project page was blocked during verification; the requested banana class names
also appear in Roboflow's official search metadata, but the source page and its
license need a manual check. No symptom facts were taken from that search.

The Kilimo class black_and_yellow_sigatoka is a combined label; it is not proof
that the app can distinguish black from yellow Sigatoka. Generic Sigatoka and
that combined label belong to one family for agreement counting. The LUIS MANUEL
page recommends v2 in prose; this implementation uses the requested /1 and does
not substitute a version. The cacao page's project metric (mAP@50 59.1%) is not
app accuracy or proof of hosted access. Br-Al's earlier spelling “black pod root”
is not treated as a separate condition from its listed black pod rot category.

Agriculture-officer review and sourced descriptions are required before adding
symptoms. Two agreeing model labels do not establish a laboratory diagnosis.
